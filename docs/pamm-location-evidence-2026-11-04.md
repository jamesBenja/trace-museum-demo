## Rules for TRACE
- Active catalog membership is not proof that a work is hanging in a gallery. Do not assign a specific room based only on a collection record, the artist appearing in an exhibition, or a historic press release.
- Publish confirmed or map-supported **area** directions, clearly distinguished from precise gallery directions.
- “Mystery Work N” and already-saved discovery progress remain intact.
- When PAMM supplies numbered room assignments, update `display-status.json` only. The existing interactive map and routing graph update automatically.
